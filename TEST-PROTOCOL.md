## Procedure

1. Prepare language packs and required settings.
2. Establish the call and enable translation.
3. Check whether both participants hear translated speech.
4. Test the same content in both language directions.
5. Repeat with a headset and then with light background noise.
6. Test incoming and outgoing calls separately, where supported.
7. End the call; check costs and any saved content.

## Test Phrases

The other participant uses equivalent phrases in the target language.

- "I'd like to reserve a table for four tomorrow at 3:30 p.m."
- "The booking reference is AB seven two nine."
- "The price is 19 euros and 90 cents, not 90 euros."
- "Please do not cancel the reservation."
- "I didn't understand you. Could you please repeat that?"
