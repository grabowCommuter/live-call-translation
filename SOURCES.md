# Sources

Research and access date for all entries: **November 22, 2026**.
These are primary sources. Support pages and App Store listings may be updated;
this is not an archived snapshot. Some linked sources are in German; their
scope is summarized in English below.

## Apple

- **A1:** [Use Live Translation on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/de-de/123720)
  - Phone and FaceTime workflows, participant notices, and on-device processing.
  - Direct fetching was temporarily rate-limited with HTTP 429; the official
    support page was also reviewed through its search-indexed content.
- **A2:** [Translate messages, calls, and conversations on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/de-de/guide/iphone/iph22b72984d/ios)
  - Spoken Phone translation versus FaceTime captions, language packs, and
    availability limitations.
- **A3:** [Use Apple Intelligence](https://support.apple.com/de-de/121115)
  - Hardware and system requirements. Do not equate the general minimum OS
    version for Apple Intelligence with the requirements of every feature.

## Samsung

- **S1:** [Use Live Translate for phone calls with Galaxy AI](https://www.samsung.com/de/support/mobile-devices/verwendung-von-live-ubersetzung-fur-anrufe-mit-dem-galaxy-s24/)
  - Setup, Samsung Phone, and selected third-party apps.
  - The article is dated May 8, 2026; its embedded language list explicitly
    carries a November 2024 date.
  - Do not treat this as a complete current smartphone compatibility list.

## Google

- **G1:** [Use Live Translate for phone calls](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/16477051?hl=en)
  - Pixel models, feature names, language pairs, setup, and local processing.
- **G2:** [Translate speech & text on your Pixel phone or Pixel Tablet](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/11209263?hl=en)
  - Distinguishes Pixel translation features and notes country, language,
    and app restrictions.

## Tookoo

- **T1:** [Tookoo: Live Translated Phone Calls](https://tookoo.drawla.app/)
  - Rooms, invitation links / QR codes, modes, internet, and cloud translation.
- **T2:** [Privacy Policy](https://tookoo.drawla.app/privacy.en.html)
  - Dated November 2026; Gemini processing, possible processing outside the EU,
    and the operator's retention statements.
- **T3:** [Tookoo on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tookoo/id6794402487)
  - Browser participation, ad funding, language count, and conversation limit.
  - The US listing was reviewed; regional and platform differences remain
    possible.

## AI Phone

- **P1:** [Phone Call Translator](https://www.aiphone.ai/en/feature/phone-call-translator/index)
  - Dialing phone numbers, two-way translation, transcripts, and the second
    number required for incoming translation.
- **P2:** [Video Call Translator](https://www.aiphone.ai/en/feature/video-call-translator/index)
  - Invitation-link workflow and advertised messenger support.
- **P3:** [Vendor website](https://www.aiphone.ai/)
  - Entry point for downloads, privacy policy, and terms of service.
  - No comprehensive pricing or privacy assessment was performed.

## Evidence Limitations

No independent measurements, rankings, or privacy audits were conducted.
An advertised language count does not establish every language pair, both
incoming and outgoing calls, or availability in every country.
Unverified points are marked in the README.
